# Code of Conduct

This project is intended for professional discussion about AI deployment readiness, governance, risk, and operational practice.

Participants should:

- be respectful;
- avoid harassment or personal attacks;
- avoid sharing private or confidential information;
- distinguish facts, assumptions, opinions, and evidence;
- avoid overstating the safety or compliance status of AI systems.

The maintainer may remove comments, issues, or pull requests that violate these expectations.
