# Method status and evidence boundary

## Status

The 70-point scale is an **author-designed decision-support heuristic**. It has not been certified, statistically calibrated, or validated as a substitute for legal, security, safety, or conformity assessment.

## What the score can do

- force a team to name workflow, data, evaluation, ownership, auditability, operations, and adoption evidence;
- make veto conditions visible before a pilot decision;
- produce a repeatable discussion artifact;
- compare changes to the same system over time when the same rubric and reviewers are used.

## What the score cannot prove

- that a system is safe, compliant, secure, fair, or production-ready;
- that two organizations use the scale consistently;
- that a higher score offsets an unresolved veto;
- that every sector-specific obligation has been assessed.

## Calibration roadmap

1. Collect at least 20 independently reviewed fictional or permission-cleared assessments.
2. Measure inter-rater agreement by category and identify ambiguous prompts.
3. Compare readiness decisions with later pilot incidents and reversals.
4. Publish rubric revisions, limitations, and migration notes.
5. Seek review from product, security, operations, legal/compliance, and affected-user representatives.

Until that work is complete, treat the output as structured evidence for a decision meeting—not the decision itself.
