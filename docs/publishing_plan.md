## Suggested social post

Many AI demos look impressive, but production deployment is different.

I created a small open toolkit for assessing whether an AI prototype is ready to move into real business workflows. It covers workflow design, data boundaries, model evaluation, human review, access control, audit logging, cost ownership, rollback, and organizational adoption.

The toolkit includes checklists, scorecards, prompts, risk templates, NIST/OWASP crosswalks, a fictional case, and a simple scoring script.
