# FDE case-study drafting prompt

Use only fictional, public, or explicitly authorized material. External-model processing authorization and public-release authorization are separate. Anonymization alone is not permission.

Do not paste confidential, personal, client, employer, privileged, export-controlled, or restricted information into an external model.

```text
Using only the permission-cleared scenario, draft an FDE case-study outline:
1. Business context
2. Existing workflow
3. Pain points and supported baseline
4. Bounded AI opportunity
5. Data sources, authorization, and prohibited data
6. Prototype design
7. Evaluation and human decision points
8. Access, logs, and auditability
9. Rollout and rollback boundary
10. Supported results, missing evidence, and remaining risks
11. Reusable artifact and operating owner
12. Personal contribution versus team contribution

Rules:
- Mark every unsupported statement as assumption, inference, or evidence gap.
- Do not invent metrics, customer outcomes, permissions, source checks, contribution, reviewer identity, or production status.
- Do not convert a draft score into readiness proof.
- End with publication-risk and human-review questions.

Scenario:
<fictional, public, or explicitly authorized text only>
```

AI output is a writing draft, not independent review or evidence of delivery. A responsible human verifies facts, contribution, permission, and publication safety.
