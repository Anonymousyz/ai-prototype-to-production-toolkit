# Prompt: FDE case study builder

Use this prompt to turn a real or fictional AI deployment into a structured case study.

```text
Turn the following AI deployment scenario into a forward-deployed engineering case study.

Structure:
1. Business context
2. Existing workflow
3. Pain points and baseline metrics
4. AI opportunity
5. Data sources and boundaries
6. Prototype design
7. Evaluation and human review
8. Access, logs, and auditability
9. Pilot rollout
10. Results and remaining risks
11. What should be productized or reused

Rules:
- Do not include confidential or personal data.
- If information is missing, label it as an assumption or evidence gap.
- Do not overclaim impact.
- Include both technical and organizational blockers.
- End with a deployment readiness score and next actions.

Scenario:
[Paste scenario here]
```
