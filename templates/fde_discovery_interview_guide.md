# FDE Discovery Interview Guide

Use this guide when interviewing a business team about a potential AI deployment.

## 1. Context

- What process are you trying to improve?
- Who performs this process today?
- How often does it happen?
- What inputs and outputs are involved?
- What makes the process painful today?

## 2. Current baseline

- How long does one cycle take?
- How many people are involved?
- What are the most common errors?
- What happens when the process fails?
- How is quality reviewed today?

## 3. AI opportunity

- Where do you think AI could help?
- What would a useful first version do?
- What should AI never do automatically?
- What output would you trust?
- What output would require human review?

## 4. Data and controls

- What data is required?
- Who owns the data?
- Is any data sensitive, personal, confidential, or regulated?
- Can the data be sent to third-party systems?
- What logs or approvals are required?

## 5. Adoption

- Who would be the first users?
- What would make them use it every day?
- What would make them reject it?
- Who will maintain it after launch?
- What would count as a successful 30-day pilot?

## 6. Evidence to collect

- workflow screenshots;
- sample inputs and outputs;
- policy or compliance constraints;
- error examples;
- approval flows;
- baseline time/cost/error data.
